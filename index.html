Account Map

One row per account you bought. Change the outcome as its story moves on: in evaluation, then passed and funded, then blown at funded. You can also rename it for its new life (for example FTMO Pro 50K-01 becomes FTMO Pro 50K-01 Funded). The path column draws the journey for you, and payouts appear on it by themselves when you pick the account on the Payouts tab.

How it counts: pass rate = accounts that ever passed divided by (ever passed + blown in evaluation). An account that passed and later blew at funded still counts as a pass, and it also shows separately as blown after passing.
